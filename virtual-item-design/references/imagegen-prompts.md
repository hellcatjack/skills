# ImageGen Prompts

Adapt one prompt to the current approved step. Replace bracketed fields with the user's choices. Use the agreed English names for items, features, views, and states. Attach actual source images and identify them by name and version. Select the projection, background, and lighting for the intended use.

## 1. Concept

> Design [Item Name], an original [Category] used by [Audience] for [Purpose]. Show one complete item in [View or State]. Its defining feature is [Shape or Graphic Motif], with [Elements and Relative Placement]. Use [Visual Style]. Frame it so the reviewer can judge [Specific Design Question] at [Viewing Context].

For a volumetric item, select an angle that shows its defining forms and connections. For a flat item, use the intended frame and face-on layout. For an effect, choose a recognizable moment and a background that reveals its boundary.

## 2. Master Sheet

> Use [Approved Concept and Version] for the identity of [Item Name]. Preserve [Defining Features]. Produce one master containing [Agreed Views, Layout, or State]. Align [Shared Landmarks] and preserve [Relative Proportions, Orientation, or Layout Anchors]. Make [Feature Under Review] easy to compare.

For newly drawn orthographic views, specify the viewing direction, the authoritative view for each shared dimension and the landmarks used to register it. Check which widths, heights and depths actually share a scale. Label any independently scaled axis. If the approved panel artwork already exists, compose the sheet from those sources. For a graphic item, specify aspect ratio, padding, and alignment. For multiple states, identify the features that stay recognizable.

## 3. Details and States

> Use [Master and Version] for [Established Shape or Layout]. Show [Detail, Surface, or State] in one image. The approved new decision is [Specific Detail or Change]. It meets or aligns with [Anchor] at [Location]. Show enough of [Surrounding Context] for the reviewer to verify that relation.

For a spacecraft underside, attach the reflected approved Top silhouette at the registered scale. Instruct ImageGen to preserve every contour break and through-hole, fill the solid footprint with visible ventral surfaces, and keep occluded dorsal hardware out of the underside artwork. Follow the silhouette check in the skill before delivering.

## 4. Individual Images

> From [Approved References], output one [View or State] of [Item Name] at [Agreed Framing and Resolution]. Match [Orientation, Extents, and Anchors] and retain [Defining Features]. Use [Background or Transparency] for [Intended Display or Handoff]. Keep the full required content inside the frame.

## 5. Local Revision

> [Approved Reference] establishes [Correct Feature]. Edit [Target Image and Version] at [Location]. Change [Observed Difference] to [Desired Result]. Preserve [Adjacent Features], [Framing], and [Remaining Appearance]. Return the complete revised image so the changed area and the overall design can both be reviewed.

For a measured revision, identify the fixed centerline or contact plane, the feature to move and the dimensions to preserve. Attach the current component in its approved pose if its outline controls the fit. Measure the named landmarks again on the saved result.

## 6. Final Appearance

> Use [Approved Master] for the form or layout of [Item Name]. Present it in [Approved View or State]. Apply [Agreed Palette and Finish] to [Named Regions]. Make [Distinguishing Feature] readable at [Intended Size or Viewing Condition]. Preserve [Approved Geometry, Graphic Boundaries, or State Cues].
